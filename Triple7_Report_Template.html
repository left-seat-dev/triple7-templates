Workflow:
1. Use the K H A P M S D C L toggle buttons at the top of each pilot section to open/close competency blocks. Closing a non-empty block prompts !! ARE YOU SURE !! - text is preserved, just hidden from the report.
2. Review and edit the text in any open box. Migrate observations between competencies by opening the target block, cutting and pasting, then closing the source.
3. To iterate one crew member at a time: use the per-pilot Send [Name] to Claude button, paste into chat, and I review just that pilot. The global Send to Claude for review at the top sends everyone at once.
4. Use the per-field Copy buttons to paste into TMS. Each click turns the button green and shows the count. The per-pilot progress bar tracks each crew member separately.
5. Before clicking submit in TMS, screenshot the page and drop it into chat for a final review.
6. Once submitted, click Save Final Record to download a date-stamped .txt archive.
Auto-sanitiser: illegal characters (em dashes, curly quotes, hidden Unicode, weird spaces) are corrected to plain keyboard characters live as you type or paste, and once more at every Copy click - what reaches TMS is always clean.
