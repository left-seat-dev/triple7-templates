    Workflow:
    1. LST rows: one card per eTags row this candidate was tested on. Locked rows show a solid code chip; rows you can change on eTags carry a dropdown, pre-set from the script and your pad choice. Grade every row. Reason chips = the competency letters that drove the grade; dashed letters are expected for the exercise, select what was actually seen. Repeats toggles 0/1 (one per item, two per test). Every row carries a comment: ultra-thin at G4, a little substantiation at G3 or G5, kept to the exercise itself (wider evidence goes to PAMS).
    2. LST RESULT and Additional Comments from Trainer: test-record lines only, no coaching.
    3. PAMS: all nine are mandatory in eTags. Grade 1-5 or NA, comment each. Then Overall Comments.
    4. The checklist above the save button lists anything eTags will refuse or a reader would query.
    5. Click Send to Claude for review, paste into the report thread for RFR. Then copy each field into eTags and click Save Final Record.
    6. One file per candidate. Edits are kept in this browser until you Clear.
